How readings work

Every card in this deck began as a card in the game. Here they are read as tarot instead.

A reading, step by step

  1. Shuffle the deck. The deck is shuffled fresh for every reading, and 21 cards are laid out face down.
  2. Choose three cards. Pick whichever ones call to you. There is no wrong choice.
  3. Watch them turn. Your cards are revealed in order. The first speaks of what has passed, the second of what stands now, and the third of what approaches.
  4. Read your fortune. A reading is written for your three cards together, tying the past, the present and what lies ahead into a single story. No two readings are the same.

Reading a card

The omen
The banner across the top holds the card’s tarot name, like The First Spark. It’s the name the reading uses.
The sign
The small square in the corner shows what kind of card it is in the game, and with it the nature of its message. The three signs are explained below.
The art
The picture is the card’s own art from the game.
The meaning
The box under the picture holds what the card means when it appears in your spread.

The three signs

The blade

Attack cards. Force, conflict and action: something must be struck, or is about to strike you.

The eye

Effect cards. Change, insight and the uncanny: things are not what they seem, and they are shifting.

The shield

Block cards. Protection, patience and endurance. These are the rarest cards in the deck.

Reversed cards

Sometimes a card turns up upside down. A reversed card shows its shadow side: the same force turned inward, gone too far, or held back. Its meaning changes to a warning, and you’ll find that meaning in the box, written the right way up. About three cards in ten come up reversed.

Readings are for fun and reflection. The deep keeps its own counsel about the future.
